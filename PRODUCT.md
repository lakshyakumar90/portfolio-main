# Portfolio

## Platform

Web, built with Next.js and React.

## Purpose and audience

Lakshya Kumar's personal portfolio presents his experience and projects to visitors evaluating his work. Visitors can scan featured projects, explore all five projects, understand their implementations, and follow source or product links.

## Confirmed constraints

- Preserve the current compact, neutral design, typography, light/dark themes, and unrelated portfolio sections.
- Feature AI Teammates, Excalidraw, and Habitly on home, in that order.
- List those projects plus Aurora UI and DevConnect at `/projects`.
- Store descriptions, links, and unlimited ordered image/video/diagram entries in typed project data; no administration interface.
- Provide accurate project information and captioned architecture diagrams. Additional media links will be supplied later.
- Support responsive layouts, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, quicker scrolling, and no cursor-following avatar.
- Preserve Habitly product, privacy, and account-deletion functionality.
